Research

Hills, S., & Beauchamp, M. T. (2024–present). Social media and disordered eating: An exploratory analysis of habits, behaviors, and beliefs when using social media. Co-Principal Investigator, Pacific University Graduate School Psychology.

This study examines how social media habits, beliefs, and behaviors relate to Eating Disorder Examination Questionnaire (EDE-Q) scores. The project includes study design, data collection, and analysis aimed at clarifying clinically relevant relationships between social media use and disordered eating patterns.

Hills, S., & Beauchamp, M. T. (2024–present). Call to action: A prescribable dose of exercise in psychology. Co-Principal Investigator, Pacific University Graduate School Psychology.

This project is a literature-based call to action advocating for more standardized and prescribable exercise metrics in psychology. It focuses on making exercise a more measurable and clinically interpretable behavioral health intervention.
