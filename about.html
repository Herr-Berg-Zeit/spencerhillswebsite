About

Graduate student in psychology at Pacific University with interests in exercise science, behavioral health, and rural mental health care.

My current work explores how exercise and related physiological variables can be described with enough precision to function as meaningful clinical tools. I am also interested in cultural competence with rural populations and the ways applied work in coaching and mental health settings can inform psychological practice.

Across research and practice, I am drawn to questions that make psychology more usable: how interventions are defined, how culture shapes care, and how practical field experience can improve clinical thinking.

Current interests

  • Exercise prescription in behavioral health
  • Rural cultural competence in mental health care
  • Applied clinical work and translational research
  • Performance, coaching, and psychological outcomes
